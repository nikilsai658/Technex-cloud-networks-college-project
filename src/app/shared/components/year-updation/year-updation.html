How Promotion Works

  • Select the From Year and To Year to move students between.
  • Leave College, Department and Branch empty to promote students across all of them.
  • Selecting a College auto-fills its code, and narrows promotion to that college only.
  • Selecting a Department or Branch further restricts which students are promoted.
